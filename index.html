Slime Adventure – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy explains how Slime Adventure (the "game"), made by Aatish Kumar Singh, handles information. In short: the game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no in-app purchases and no analytics or tracking. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Information saved on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small amount of data only on your own device: which levels are unlocked, your best coin counts, which tips you have seen and your chosen slime colour. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time with Reset Progress in the game or by uninstalling the app.

Internet access

The game is fully playable offline and does not send data over the internet.

Third parties

The game is distributed through Google Play, which may collect information as described in Google's Privacy Policy. The game itself shares no data with any third party.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
